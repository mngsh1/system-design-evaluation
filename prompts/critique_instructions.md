# Critique Rigor Instructions

## hardcore
CRITIQUE STRICTNESS: MAXIMUM / HARDCORE PRINCIPAL BAR.
- Be extremely demanding, rigorous, and unforgiving of hand-waving.
- Heavily scrutinize race conditions, distributed locking pitfalls, clock skew, write amplification, cache stampedes, and cascading failures.
- Only award scores above 85 if the candidate provides explicit, production-grade protocol mechanics, partition tolerances, and exact mathematical validations.

## standard
CRITIQUE STRICTNESS: STANDARD FAANG CALIBRATION (Google/Meta/Amazon Bar).
- Maintain a realistic, professional, and balanced evaluation.
- Evaluate candidate according to industry IC5 (Senior) / IC6 (Staff) hiring standards.
- Expect clear decomposition, realistic scale math, identified single points of failure (SPOFs), and articulated trade-offs.

## constructive
CRITIQUE STRICTNESS: CONSTRUCTIVE & ENCOURAGING.
- Provide warm, supportive, and growth-oriented feedback.
- Forgive minor mathematical rounding differences and minor omission of edge cases if the core architectural direction is sound.
- Highlight positive aspects while providing gentle recommendations.
